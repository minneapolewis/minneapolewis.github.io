About the Temple Archive

This system was designed to index and archive files and videos, making them fully searchable via an Optical Character Recognition (OCR) pipeline.


The interface pays homage to the design philosophy of TempleOS, prioritizing raw utilitarian layouts, unpretentious controls, and 16-color VGA aesthetics.
